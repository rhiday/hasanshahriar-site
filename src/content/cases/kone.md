KONE makes some of the most physical products in the world. My work sat at the opposite end of that reality: turning complex systems into digital experiences people could understand, operate and act on.

The relationship covered two distinct engagements. The first was **SiteFlow**, a product and demand-generation surface for construction teams. The second was a custom visualisation environment built around live building data.

## The problem behind the interface

An elevator, hoist or construction flow is not a neat row in a database. It is a moving system inside another moving system: a building under construction, people changing floors, equipment changing state, and different teams needing different levels of detail.

The digital layer had to make that complexity legible without making it feel small.

## What I worked on

On SiteFlow, I worked across the web experience, conversion paths and the operational details required to ship inside a global enterprise environment.

The deeper technical engagement was a **Three.js and Vue-based building visualisation dashboard**. It represented elevators, hoists and jump-lifts inside a navigable 3D environment, synchronised with KONE data. The wider exploration also included live people avatars, service views and a Mapbox-based globe.

My role moved between implementation, product judgement and translation: understanding what the system was doing, finding the part a user actually needed to see, and turning it into an interface that could survive contact with real operations.

## The operating lesson

Enterprise work is often described as a scale problem. In practice, it is a coordination problem.

The code has to work, but so do the handoffs, permissions, design standards, terminology and release path. The useful skill is not merely building the interface. It is keeping the interface coherent while the number of people around it grows.

That lesson has followed me into every product since: complexity does not disappear because the UI is clean. A good interface simply gives the complexity somewhere sensible to live.
