# Content queue

How this works: new essays land in `src/content/essays/` with `draft: true` in the frontmatter. They do **not** appear on the live site until you read one, decide it's you, and change `draft: true` → `draft: false` (or delete the line). That's the review gate. Publishing is one keystroke; nothing goes out in your name unread.
