## ✅ LOCKED DECISIONS (the spine)

### 1. Positioning / angle
- **Primary job of the site:** founder-as-peer **reputation engine**. The writing is the product; reputation is the outcome; the projects are proof, never the pitch.
- **One-liner (live in header):** *A founder building far from the center — essays on shipping, instinct, and the outsider's edge.*
- **Three content pillars:** **The Outsider's Edge** (signature) · **Sent, Not Saved** (most shareable) · **Building in the AI Era** (thinnest; feed next).
- **Ownable phrase:** **"Sent, Not Saved"** — verb, value, and cure in three words. Thread it everywhere.
- **Reachable target:** ~**1,000 true readers** in the outsider-builder niche. Not a vanity number. (Anti-freeze: a target that can be judged.)
- **Anti-positioning:** no tips-blog, no LinkedIn-motivation voice, never anonymous, don't lead with the projects.
- → detail: `NORTH-STAR.md`

### 2. Distribution
- **Site-first, syndicate to Substack.** hasanshahriar.com is canonical (owns SEO, design, archive). Substack = email + distribution layer. One newsletter for now; topic newsletters (Copyblogger-style) parked for later.
- → detail: `NORTH-STAR.md`

### 3. Format / storytelling
- Keep the **deep long essays** (the moat) but add a **scannable layer**: TL;DR up top, pull-quotes, subheads. Atomize rather than shorten.
- **Visual signature:** the **"napkin sketch"** (Tim Urban move) — one deliberately-rough hand drawing per piece. Cheap, recognizable, no design skills needed. Validated with 3 samples.
- → detail: `NORTH-STAR.md`, `content-os.md`

### 4. Homepage redesign
- **Angle:** *"Founder in the arena, writing from the edge."* In 10 seconds the visitor sees: he builds real things (authority) + has a voice/story (personality) + I can follow (newsletter).
- **Structure** (borrowed from Guillaume Moubeche / Ali Abdaal, tone kept literary): hero (photo + intro + inline subscribe) → proof strip → what I'm building (3 cards) → featured essays → pillars → light journey → newsletter band.
- **Guardrail:** authority via **concrete specifics** (City of Helsinki pilot, KONE), NOT vanity metrics. Faking guru-energy would kill the honesty the brand runs on.
- **Essays move to `/essays`;** homepage becomes the landing.
- **Open calls:** photo in hero (recommended yes) · primary CTA Read vs Subscribe (lean Read) · how prominent a Makeshift link (keep subtle).
- → detail: `homepage-proposal.md` + visual `homepage-mockup.html`

### 5. Content engine — branded **RECEIPTS**
- **Throughline POV:** *When building is free, the winners aren't the best builders — they're the ones who send. The outsider who ships beats the insider who polishes.* (AI is the hook, shipping is the topic.)
- **Brand label:** **RECEIPTS** — building in the open, from the edge; proof, not vanity.
- **Three recurring formats:** **THE SEND** (honest build-journeys — "shipped X in Y, here's the messy part") · **FROM THE EDGE** (immigrant/outsider lens; where the LinkedIn drafts belong) · **THE MOAT MOVED** (his take on something recent).
- 9 starter posts drafted from real material.
- → detail: `content-engine.md`

### 6. Content OS — managed, not random
- **Lifecycle:** Capture → Draft (site, canonical) → Atomize → Publish (X + LinkedIn first → Substack → site → talk) → Repurpose.
- **Management:** one board, 4 columns — *Backlog → Drafting → Ready to send → Published/repurpose*. Each card carries a **format label** (SEND/EDGE/MOAT), a **channel checklist**, and **sketch status**. Tooling: **Notion** (connected) — board not yet built.
- **Anti-freeze rules:** a card can't sit in "Ready" >7 days (ships as-is); atomize capped at 20 min; one idea per piece.
- → detail: `content-os.md`

### 7. Visual social channel — Instagram (+ TikTok)
- **IG fits** (visual moat + 2026 carousels drive ~22% more saves). **Rule: not a new content stream** — same RECEIPTS, different shelf. TikTok/Reels = repurpose, not new work.
- **Set-up:** `@hasanshahriar` everywhere; bio funnels to site; SEND/EDGE/MOAT as story highlights; 4:5 portrait; 3 reusable templates (quote / sketch / carousel).
- **Measure** saves, shares, DMs, profile→link clicks — not followers.
- **Open call:** on-camera for Reels or sketches-only?
- → detail: `receipts-instagram-tiktok.md`
