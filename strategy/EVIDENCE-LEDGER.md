# Public evidence ledger

*Created 2026-07-25 for the trust-artifact redesign. This is the publishing safety layer: specific enough to be credible, conservative enough to defend.*

## Publishing rules

- Public client and partner names are approved.
- Do not publish internal dashboards, private inboxes, pipeline data, customer data or confidential screenshots.
- Prefer public-safe reconstruction and original artwork over private delivery artefacts.
- A claim can ship when it is backed by the client sweep, repository evidence, a public source or Hasan's explicit confirmation.
- If a number is useful but not evidenced, remove the number. Specific systems and decisions carry more trust than decorative metrics.
