*How the napkin sketches become a visual-native presence that feeds the site/newsletter. Pairs with content-os.md. Built: 2026-06-30.*

---

## The honest push-back first

You now have X, LinkedIn, Substack, the site, a possible talk — and now IG + TikTok. That's a lot of mouths to feed, and you of all people know what happens when you spread across too many bets: nothing gets the whole of you, the freeze hides in the gaps. So the rule for this channel:

**Instagram is not a new content stream. It's a different shelf for the same RECEIPTS you already make.** You write the essay once, atomize it once, and the carousel that goes to IG is the *same* carousel concept you'd post on LinkedIn. TikTok is a *repurpose* of that, not new work. One visual system, posted to the place built for visuals. If it ever starts to feel like a separate job, we cut it.

That said — IG genuinely fits you better than most channels, because your moat is *visual* (the napkin sketches) and IG is where visual-first ideas travel. The data backs it: in 2026 carousels drive ~22% more saves, and **saves + DM-shares are exactly what the algorithm rewards** — i.e. the "screenshot-this" lines you already write naturally.

## The visual system (so every post is on-brand and fast)

- **Palette:** paper `#fbfbf9`, ink `#1a1a1a`, terracotta `#9a3b2e`, one cool accent `#3f6b8a`. Never more.
- **Three tile types**, rotated: (1) **napkin sketch** post, (2) **quote card** (terracotta, white serif), (3) **carousel** (hook → idea → sketch → CTA).
- **Format:** 4:5 portrait (takes the most feed space). Serif for the words, the rough hand for the drawings.
- **Template, not design.** Build 3 reusable templates once; after that a post is fill-in-the-blanks, ~15 min. No designing per post — that's the freeze trap wearing an apron.

## TikTok / Reels — the repurpose layer (don't overthink it)

Same content, two cheap motions:
1. **Animated carousel** — the swipe slides as a 15–20s vertical video with a hooky caption. Tools do this automatically; zero new writing.
2. **Talking-head over the sketch** — you read the hook to camera with the napkin drawing behind you, 20–30s. This is where your *voice and face* (the authority + personality the homepage is also chasing) do double duty. One recording → IG Reel + TikTok + a clip for X.

Reels are for **discovery** (reaching people who don't follow you); carousels are for **depth** (converting the ones who do). Use both, but never let TikTok become a separate script.

---

## Cadence & what to measure

- **3–4 posts/week**, all atomized from that week's RECEIPTS — e.g. 1 carousel, 1 quote card, 1 sketch, 1 Reel. No extra writing; it's all cut from the essay.
- **Measure saves, shares, DMs, profile→link clicks** — not followers. Your goal is the same: the right small audience that ends up on the site and the newsletter. Likes are vanity; saves are intent.
